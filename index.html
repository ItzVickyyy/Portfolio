About me

Still learning, already building.

I'm a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Laguna State Polytechnic University — Los Baños Campus, currently a Dean's Lister for two consecutive academic years. Before college, I took the Information and Communication Technology (ICT) strand in senior high school, which is where I first got hooked on building things for the web.

Outside the classroom, I currently serve as Recording Secretary and Head of the Plans and Events Committee for the College of Computer Studies Student Council, and earlier as the ICT Representative and Media Club Multimedia Manager back in senior high. I like being useful to a team as much as I like writing code — planning events taught me a lot of the same problem-solving instincts that debugging does.

Right now I'm focused on full-stack web development and UI/UX design, and slowly rounding that out with systems and network administration fundamentals from my NC II training. I'm still early in this journey, but every project below is something I actually built, start to finish.
